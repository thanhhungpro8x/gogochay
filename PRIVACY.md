# Privacy policy — GoGoChay

GoGoChay does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data.

* **No network requests.** The extension never contacts any server. Ad blocking is done by the browser itself, from rule lists bundled in the extension.
* **Only your own settings are stored, on your device** (browser extension storage): the sites where you paused GoGoChay, the elements you hid with "Hide an element…" (one CSS selector per site), the interface language, the aggressive-blocking setting and the sites where you turned it off, the fake-address warning setting, and the addresses you marked as trusted. They never leave your device and are deleted when you remove the extension.
* **The tray** shows information about ads hidden on the current page (source host, size, text). It stays in your browser and is discarded when the tray closes.
* **The fake-address check** runs entirely in your browser: it looks at the address of the page you are on and compares it with a list built into the extension. No address is sent anywhere.
